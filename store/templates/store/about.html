Refund, Compensation & Reimbursement Rules

Review standard policies and guidelines for handling flight cancellations, delays, disrupted journeys, and associated expenses.

Refund Policy

Travelers can request a full cash refund for cancelled or heavily disrupted flights quickly and securely. This covers tickets affected by unexpected airline schedule changes or operational cancellations.

Compensation Guidelines

Check your flight disruption details and calculate your compensation entitlements instantly up to €600. Compensation availability depends on the timing of notification, length of delay at the final destination, and operational causes.

  • Applicable for extended delays, cancellations without prior notice, and denied boarding situations.
  • Entitlements scale based on distance and total arrival delay time.

Reimbursement & Expenses

Submit receipts for meals, accommodation, and transport expenses incurred due to your flight delay. When disruptions force overnight waits or prolonged airport stays, eligible passengers are entitled to standard provisions of care.

  • Hotel accommodations and airport transfer coverage during overnight delays.
  • Food, beverage, and communication vouchers issued according to waiting time thresholds.
